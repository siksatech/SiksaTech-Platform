# 65. CHILD DATA / PRIVACY

Because SiksaTech targets Class 5 onward, child data protection is a first-class architectural requirement.

The product must be designed to comply with the applicable Indian data-protection framework.

For users who are children:

* establish age handling
* implement verifiable parental consent where required
* maintain parent-child relationships
* provide appropriate notices
* minimize collection
* provide deletion/access workflows
* restrict inappropriate tracking
* do not use targeted advertising directed at children
* do not use behavioral monitoring contrary to applicable law
* document processing purposes

The DPDP Act specifically requires verifiable parental consent for processing children's personal data and restricts detrimental processing, tracking/behavioural monitoring and targeted advertising directed at children, subject to the Act's specified exceptions.

The final implementation must follow the current notified DPDP Act, Rules and applicable exemptions/implementation dates rather than relying on this PRD alone.
